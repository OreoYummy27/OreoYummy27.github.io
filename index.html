A Bill's Life

The Government

The Steps

House

Senate

Any member can introduce a bill at any time while the House is in session. The bill is placed in the "hopper" (a wooden box on the House floor). The sponsor's signature must appear on the bill. Bills introduced in the House are prefixed with "H.R.".

In the Senate, a bill is typically introduced by a Senator presenting it to a clerk at the presiding officer's desk. The Senator does not typically comment on the bill from the floor at this stage. The bill is then assigned a number, like "S.".

Committee

Committee

The House rules the committee and the debate can be limited by rules and the debates must list be germane. Committee of the whole will consider the bill, as well as debating and voting on it. They will vote by electronic device.

Ammendments must be germane and will only be brought to the floor by unanimous consent. There is an open debate and not limited, the fillibuster ended by a cloture motion with 60 senators agreeing to end debate. They can only limit it by unanimous consent. Vote by roll call.

Compromises

They will debate on each of their versions and each chamber will have to seperately pass it. There is a Conference Committee where they all debate and decide on everything for a version.

President

Then it will be sent to the president and he will decide what he wants to do with it.

Pass

Veto

Pocket Veto

If he passes within 10 days then it will become an ammendment right away.

If he vetoes the bill then Congress can still overide his veto by two thirds of each chamber voting and it can still become and ammendment.

If he deoesn't do anything and Congress is still in session then the law immediately becomes an ammendment, but if Congress isn't in session then it will automically get vetoed.
